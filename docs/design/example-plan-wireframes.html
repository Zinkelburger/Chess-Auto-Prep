D2 · How far do we ask?

A · Ask until the book runs out

2.c4 →3.Nc3 Nf6 4.Bg5 →5.e3 O-O 6.Nf3 →7.Rc1 c6 8.Bd3 →book ends
4–7 questions per branch; thorough, slower.

+ Every real decision is yours. − Long walk for a wide repertoire (5 branches × 5 forks).

B · Same, but “stop here” on every card my lean

2.c4 →3.Nc3 Nf6 4.Bg5 →Stop here — generate from 4…Be7
2 answers and go; the engine + your setup preference fill the rest. A depth counter shows what is still open.

+ Fast path for the impatient, full path for the careful. − Two exits on every card — needs restraint in the design.

D3 · Prefill from your own games?

A · Existing chapters only

Choices already in the repertoire are shown as “in your chapters” and skipped. Nothing else is inferred. Clean and predictable.

B · Also your games (opt-in) my lean

If Player Analysis has your accounts, each fork shows “you played …c6 here in 40 of 52 games” and pre-ticks it. Real evidence, no guessing; a toggle on the Start screen, off by default.
